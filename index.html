01 — Intro

Nami is a house
for slowness.

Twelve saunas, four waters and one long exhale, five minutes from the sea. We keep the lights low, the voices lower, and the phones outside. What happens here is simple — heat, cold, rest, repeat — until the body remembers itself.

— ნამი, dew. The water that arrives without a sound.

900 candles, lit at dusk.

02 — The House

Warm walnut,
black river basalt,
unlacquered brass.

Nami sits inside a former customs warehouse on the old port line, six minutes from Batumi Boulevard. We kept the 1912 brick and let everything else go quiet: oiled walnut, basalt lifted from the Chorokhi riverbed, linen the colour of wet sand, brass that is allowed to age.

Nothing here is polished to a shine. Warmth is the only decoration — a strip of light behind a screen, a fire that is never fully out, steam that finds the ceiling and comes back down as rain.
